This Week in Charleston

A weekly video recap of what the City of Charleston's boards, commissions and council actually did.

What it is

Each week this project reviews the City of Charleston's published public meeting recordings and its AgendaCenter — agendas, staff reports and minutes — and produces a short video summarising the decisions that affect residents: rezonings, variances, contracts and budget items.

Where the information comes from

  • City of Charleston public meeting recordings published on YouTube
  • The City of Charleston AgendaCenter (agendas, staff reports, minutes)
  • City of Charleston news releases
  • Imagery from Google Street View, Google Maps and Google Aerial View

Meeting captions are machine-generated and may contain errors. The official record is the city's own video and minutes at charleston-sc.gov/AgendaCenter.

Use of the YouTube API

This application uses the YouTube Data API solely to upload finished episodes to its own YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or analyse any other account's data. See the privacy policy.

Independent project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of Charleston.
