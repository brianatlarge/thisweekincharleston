Privacy Policy

This Week in Charleston — last updated 23 August 2026.

Summary

This application has no users other than its operator. It collects no personal information from anyone, has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking. It publishes videos to a single YouTube channel owned by the operator.

What data the application accesses

The application requests one Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which allows it to upload video files to the operator's own YouTube channel. It uses this only to publish its own weekly episodes, always as private videos. It does not read, modify, download or analyse any other content, and it does not access any other person's Google or YouTube account.

What is stored, and where

An OAuth refresh token issued by Google is stored on the operator's own server so that scheduled uploads can run without re-authorising. It is not shared with anyone and is not transmitted anywhere except to Google when requesting a new access token. No other Google account data is stored.

Information in the videos

Episodes are assembled from public records: City of Charleston public meeting recordings, published agendas, staff reports and minutes, and city news releases. They may therefore include the names and statements of people who spoke on the record at a public meeting, and images of properties discussed at those meetings. This is information the City of Charleston has already published. To request a correction, contact the operator at the address below.

Third parties

The application sends meeting transcripts and agenda text to Anthropic (analysis and scriptwriting) and narration text to ElevenLabs (speech synthesis), and retrieves imagery from Google Maps Platform. It does not send any personal information about viewers to these services, because it collects none.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
