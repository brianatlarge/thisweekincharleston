1. What this is

This Week in Charleston is an independent, automated project that produces a weekly video summary of public meetings held by the City of Charleston, South Carolina. It is operated by a private individual. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official communication of the City of Charleston or any of its boards, commissions or departments.

2. The application itself has no public users

The software described here is operated solely by its owner to publish videos to a single YouTube channel that the owner controls. There is no sign-up, no account, and no way for a third party to log in to or use the application. These terms exist to satisfy Google's OAuth requirements and to set expectations for anyone who watches the videos it produces.

3. Accuracy, and what to rely on

Episodes are assembled automatically from public records, including machine-generated meeting captions that are known to contain errors in names, addresses, dollar figures and vote counts. Summaries are generated by software and may misstate or omit things.

Do not rely on these videos for any legal, financial, property or civic decision. The authoritative record is the City of Charleston's own meeting video, agendas and minutes, published at charleston-sc.gov/AgendaCenter. Where a video and the official record disagree, the official record governs.

4. Corrections
